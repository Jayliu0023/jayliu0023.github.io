Keeping data leakage out of evaluation

Protect the boundary between information available during training and during evaluation.

This sample demonstrates the article format for this blog.

Leakage can be subtle

Evaluation becomes misleading when training or model selection uses information that would not be available when making real predictions. Duplicated examples, target-derived features, and preprocessing fitted on the full dataset are common sources.

Split according to the task

A random split is not always appropriate. Related samples may need to stay in the same group, and forecasting tasks typically require a time-based split. The split should reflect the intended use of the model.

Audit the full pipeline

Check feature construction, normalization, imputation, and parameter selection as well as the estimator itself. Fit learned preprocessing only within the training data for each split, and reserve the test set for the final evaluation.
