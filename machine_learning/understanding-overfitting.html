Understanding overfitting

Why a model can fit its training data well and still struggle with new examples.

This sample demonstrates the article format for this blog.

Training performance is only one view

A model can learn patterns that are specific to its training set, including noise. When those patterns do not hold in new data, excellent training performance can coexist with poor generalization.

Use validation curves

Compare training and validation performance as training progresses or model complexity changes. A widening gap can suggest overfitting, but distribution differences and a small validation set can also affect the result.

Choose an appropriate remedy

Regularization, early stopping, simpler models, and better training data can help. Choose settings using validation data and keep the final test set separate so that repeated decisions do not turn it into another training signal.
