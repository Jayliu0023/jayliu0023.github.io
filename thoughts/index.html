Why The West Rules, For Now - Ian Morris
In the middle of the eighteenth century, the West started checking out. Why did the West rule? And will it last? Ian Morris delivers a fascinating account of human history through the lens of geography and social development.
The Selfish Gene - Richard Dawkins
We are survival machines—robot vehicles blindly programmed to preserve the selfish molecules known as genes. This book changed the way we look at evolution and natural selection.
Deep Learning - Ian Goodfellow et al.
The bible of Deep Learning. It covers the mathematical and conceptual background, deep learning techniques used in industry, and research perspectives. Essential for understanding modern AI.
Sapiens: A Brief History of Humankind - Yuval Noah Harari
Destined to become a modern classic, Harari challenges everything we know about being human: our thoughts, our actions, our power... and our future.
Molecular Biology of the Cell - Alberts et al.
The classic reference text for cell biology. It provides a comprehensive understanding of the molecular mechanisms that govern cell function and structure.
